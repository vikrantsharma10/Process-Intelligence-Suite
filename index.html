What is this

A suite of diagnostic tools built for operations too complex to fix with instinct alone.

PIS is not a project management tool. It doesn't track tasks or generate reports. It runs a structured diagnosis on your operational processes and hands you back two things; a version your leadership can act on and a version your team can action immediately.

Each module targets a different layer of the same problem. You can use one or all three. The output is the same either way; a clear picture of what's broken, who owns the break, and what to fix first.

What it diagnoses
Process health, not people performance

Every output is structured around the process itself; steps, handoffs, ownership, and time gaps. Not who dropped the ball.

Who it's for
Two outputs per module; two audiences

Leadership gets board-ready commercial framing. The team gets a ranked, operational fix list. Same diagnosis; different language.

How fast
Under 60 seconds. No consultant required

How it works

Three inputs.
One verdict.

Step 01
Describe your process

No templates. No structured forms on Module 01. Write it how you'd explain it to a colleague; include role names, tools, and where it typically breaks. Modules 02 and 03 use a table; one row per step.

Step 02
The engine runs

Depending on the module; the engine classifies steps, calculates time gaps, runs CAGE framework validation, or maps ownership. No user input required after you hit go.

Step 03
Two outputs, two audiences

Every module produces two layers. One for leadership; sharp, board-ready, commercial. One for the team; operational, ranked, immediately actionable.

Step 04
Act on it

The output tells you what to fix, in what order, and why. Not a list of symptoms. A sequenced diagnosis with effort and impact framing built in.
